# Journey artwork

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool for the approved portfolio plan.
The source asset is `journey.png`. It contains seven illustrated scenes.
The website uses SVG viewports to display each scene without extra image files.
The photos supplied by the user informed the setting. They are not published.

## Generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create one tall editorial illustration atlas, exactly SEVEN equal-height horizontal panels stacked vertically, no gaps, no borders, no text. Each panel is a wide 3:2 landscape illustration. Warm hand-drawn gouache and coloured pencil on ivory paper, muted turquoise, terracotta, olive green, soft ink contours, sophisticated personal memoir. Consistent Indian male protagonist, black hair, brown skin, rectangular glasses as adult. Panel 1: small child sitting with an adult on black vintage Rajdoot motorcycle in Maharashtra village courtyard, turquoise plaster house, dark carved wooden pillars, stone veranda, tiled roof, white bulls and cream dog. Panel 2: boy reading open notebook on same veranda, school bag nearby. Panel 3: schoolboy riding step-through ladies bicycle along village lane, distant walking child to suggest earlier walking route. Panel 4: young man on black commuter motorcycle with pink milk can attached, leaving same turquoise village gate for milk delivery. Panel 5: adult with glasses and helmet riding grey Royal Enfield with brown seat through green hills near reservoir, echo motorcycle composition from panel 1. Panel 6: man on camel in ochre desert, small passenger airplane high in sky. Panel 7: adult with glasses at desk with laptop and monitor, guitar leaning nearby, turquoise wall accent and warm lamp. Retain village-specific architecture and everyday lived-in details. Not photorealistic. Each panel complete and separated by composition only; all seven equal heights essential for website sprite use. No typography, no labels, no watermark.

## Review limits

The artwork represents memories. It does not reproduce exact faces or vehicle specifications.
Scroll moves each scene and its progress indicator. Characters do not have separate animated limbs.
